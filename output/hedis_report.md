# HEDIS Public Intelligence Report — Ohio

> Public data only (Medicaid Core Set, CMS MIPS, CDC PLACES). Fetched 2026-11-01.

## Summary

- Ohio Medicaid reports 15 of 16 tracked HEDIS-based measures in the 2025 Core Set: 2 in the top quartile of states, 2 below the median.
- Top priority: WCV Child & Adolescent Well-Care Visits: 49.9% vs top quartile 58.8% (8.9 pts below the top quartile; ~95,097 more members needed to reach it; +5.4 pts over 2021–2025).
- Reaching the top quartile on every measure with a published denominator would take ~135,418 more members meeting the measure.
- Biggest provider-group opportunity: Depression Screening & Follow-Up (MIPS #134): 2,820,285 patients not meeting across 99 groups.
- Largest community need: Cuyahoga County: ~131,639 adults with diabetes.
- 6 data-quality checks flagged on the public sources.

## What to work on first

**1. Focus measure: WCV Child & Adolescent Well-Care Visits**
- Why: below median (#32 of 52 states); 8.9 pts below the top quartile; ~95,097 more members needed to reach it; +5.4 pts over 2021–2025
- Action: Align plans, providers and outreach on this measure first.

**2. Colorectal screening push**
- Why: adults in these counties × unscreened share ≈ 650,416 (rough upper bound)
- Action: FIT-kit mailers and community screening events in Franklin, Butler, Trumbull.

**3. Depression Screening & Follow-Up: group partnership**
- Why: ~1,267,734 patients not meeting the measure
- Action: Start with Cleveland Clinic.

**4. Close data gaps**
- Why: Measures Ohio did not report in the latest release: COL; Measures reported by fewer than 40 states (weaker comparison): GSD (38)
- Action: Plan for the internal data public sources cannot provide.

## Not available in public data

- **Which individual patients should we contact?** Public data has no patient-level records. Needs: Patient-level gap extract (PatientIdentityKey, MeasureCode, PayerStatus, EpicStatus, LastVisitDate, ...).
- **Which gaps are closed in Epic but still open with the payer?** Payer and EHR gap statuses are not published. Needs: PayerStatus and EpicStatus per patient-measure.
- **How are commercial and Medicare Advantage HEDIS rates performing?** Plan-level HEDIS results (NCQA Quality Compass) are licensed, not a public API. Needs: Plan HEDIS results or your own payer reports.
- **What are our contract targets and incentive dollars?** Contract terms are private. Needs: Payer contract targets and incentive schedules.
- **Which of our own providers and clinics are behind?** Public data covers provider groups that report to CMS MIPS, not your attribution. Needs: Your attribution (ProviderId, DepartmentName) joined to your gap data.
